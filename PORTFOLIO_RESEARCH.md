## Implemented direction

- Existing identity and supplied artwork retained, with a clearer hero statement.
- Three featured project stories followed by a filterable nine-project collection.
- Professional banking and HRMS contributions use only facts already present in the portfolio.
- Both themes, initial system preference, persistent manual selection, accessible toggle and early initialization to avoid the wrong theme flashing.
- Professional contribution details expand with native keyboard-accessible disclosures.

## Additional SaaS case studies — content required

For each project supply: public name or anonymous title, employer/client attribution that may be shown, product purpose and users, dates, exact role, personally delivered modules, technology, engineering decisions/tradeoffs, verified results, and public links or approved screenshots.

Do not infer SaaS architecture, tenancy, revenue, user counts, ownership, or measured improvements from the existing employment descriptions. The new professional section is not a claim that those applications were SaaS products.
